## USER ARRIVES BECAUSE
A Founder or future public user needs to understand one real object quickly — species, place, living system, actor, solution, signal or proof — without learning the internal 4PLANET architecture first. Magazine readers arrive because a story is worth reading, not because they want a product-system explanation.

## ONE THING TO UNDERSTAND
There is **one 4PLANET identity, but not one rigid universal profile layout**. Shared tokens, navigation, evidence semantics, interaction and source grammar create coherence. Species, Place, Living System, Actor, Solution, Signal and Proof retain distinct information hierarchies because their user jobs and truth shapes differ.

## PRIMARY ACTION
On every surface, the primary action is to understand the object/story and take the single most useful next step: explore a connected object, open a source, follow the relevant place/species/actor, or continue into the 4PLANET system. No page should force the reader through internal methodology before delivering value.

## SECONDARY DEPTH
Sources, provenance, limitations, UNKNOWN states and additional context remain immediately available, but move behind compact progressive disclosure where they would otherwise dominate the public reading experience. Truth does not disappear when interface noise disappears.

## P1 DOMINANT
The dominant layer is the object/story itself: its identity, human relevance and strongest visual or spatial idea. One primary visual gesture per viewport. Instrument Sans display, DM Sans reading/UI, Fragment Mono evidence/meta. Default surfaces are pure white and ink/black with BRANDBLÅ `#2E2EFF` as the shared system accent.

## P2 ORIENTATION
Orientation is compact and contextual: object/story type, status only when useful, essential location/time/range context, and a clear standfirst. Internal labels such as donor lineage, architecture notes and template philosophy must not compete with the object.

## P3 ACTION / NEXT
Connected objects and next actions appear after the user has received value. They are concise rows or contextual controls, not generic SaaS cards. Magazine stories end by opening the world — related Species, Place, Living System, Actor, Solution, Signal or Proof — rather than trapping the reader inside article architecture.

## P4 DEPTH
Deep evidence includes source records, checked-at state, claim boundary, relationship state, method detail, uncertainty and limitations. It is available through compact drawers/expansion and source links. PROOF keeps claim → evidence → method → confidence → uncertainty explicit. SIGNAL keeps event → time → authority → unresolved outcome explicit.

## WHAT CAN BE REMOVED
Remove or suppress from the primary experience: warm beige/grey background surfaces; random serif/editorial font drift; repeated giant manifesto headings; full-screen truth-boundary statements; internal template/job/system copy; mini-preview/debug thumbnails; decorative status chips; redundant locked blocks; generic card walls; duplicated explanations; visual elements without reader value. Do not remove evidence or truth boundaries — compress them.

## TRUTH BOUNDARY
Unknown stays unknown. Decision ≠ delivery ≠ ecological outcome. Activity is not impact. SIGNAL is temporal evidence, not outcome truth. PROOF refuses unsupported success claims. Blue Whale may reuse ORCA capability patterns but not Orca-specific claims. Actor review remains DEVELOPMENT until Founder approval. Review media must be designed in-system or otherwise rights-cleared; no unverified documentary media dependency is allowed. The TEST review carries no LIVE authority and production LIVE remains Founder-gated until explicit `ENIG LIVE`.

## MOBILE-FIRST RISK
Primary mobile risks are oversized headings, long metadata stacks, two-column assumptions, evidence rows that overflow, and visual modules whose meaning depends on desktop width. Mobile must preserve hierarchy and source access at 390/430 widths with shorter titles, single-column progression and touch-safe disclosure controls. Mobile quality must equal desktop quality.

The interface feels calm, premium, scientific, editorial and unmistakably 4PLANET — not SaaS, NGO, ESG dashboard or internal prototype. Founder review should reveal materially distinct Species/Place/Living System/Actor/Solution/Signal/Proof grammars while still feeling like one product family.
