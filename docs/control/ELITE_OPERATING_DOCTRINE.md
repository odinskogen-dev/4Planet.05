## 1. Programme admission

Before a new major programme, permanent system, product family, large architecture expansion or other high-recursive-cost workstream is activated, answer in plain language:

- What are we trying to achieve?
- How is it done now?
- What is materially new?
- Who receives material value and why do they care?
- Why now?
- What are the principal risks?
- What cost/complexity does it create?
- What are the observable midterm and final examinations?

If the initiative cannot pass, `DELETE / DEFER / REDUCE` before WBS, new architecture or automation is created.

## 2. Human Gold Backwards

Before substantial user-facing work, extend the existing GOLD Current Brief by resolving:

- WHO arrives;
- WHAT job/problem they have;
- WHAT becomes possible or materially better;
- WHAT they must understand;
- PRIMARY action;
- WHAT proof demonstrates value;
- WHY they return;
- WHAT world-class success looks like.

Do not create a second product-spec system.

## 3. Simplest capable execution

Escalate only when necessary:

`DETERMINISTIC CODE / EXISTING PRIMITIVE`
→ `SIMPLE WORKFLOW`
→ `LLM WORKFLOW`
→ `SINGLE AGENT`
→ `MULTI-AGENT`.

More agents are not more progress. Optimise for minimum coordination cost per accepted Human Gold / verified value loop.

## 4. Decision reversibility

- `TWO-WAY DOOR`: reversible at bounded cost. May be delegated inside current Canon and authority.
- `ONE-WAY DOOR`: difficult, expensive or externally binding to reverse. Retains the existing Founder/professional gate.

Production release, Canon change, external send, signing, payment/financial commitment, irreversible deletion, public impact claim and high-risk legal/accounting/rights/security decisions are one-way by default unless an explicit current authority says otherwise.

## 5. Jidoka / Andon — fail closed

Stop mutation or promotion when any material abnormality is unresolved:

- authority ambiguity;
- stale/current-state conflict;
- source-isolation breach;
- protected-Gold or lineage-loss risk;
- regression of a protected invariant;
- unsupported truth/claim promotion;
- secret/security exposure;
- destructive uncertainty;
- scope/permission breach;
- critical evaluator failure;
- writeback/readback mismatch;
- rollback identity unavailable.

Required response: `STOP → PRESERVE STATE/EVIDENCE → CLASSIFY → CORRECT → REGRESSION TEST → RESUME`.

## 6. Autonomy Budget

Autonomy is earned from measured reliability, not confidence or activity. Track where applicable:

- accepted-first-pass rate;
- safe fail-closed interceptions;
- material regression rate;
- recovery time;
- authority/source violations;
- writeback fidelity;
- Founder correction burden;
- accepted-value throughput.

Source-isolation breaches remain zero-tolerance. Do not invent fixed numeric thresholds before a real baseline exists. Reliability outside the approved envelope freezes autonomy expansion and shifts work to reliability until re-proven.

## 7. Maker is not sole judge

For material truth, source, security, release, impact, acceptance and irreversible gates, the producer may not be the sole verifier. Use technically/organisationally independent review proportional to risk.

- Verification: was the artifact built correctly?
- Validation: was the right artifact/value built?
- Founder judgement: remains separate where taste/release authority applies.

## 8. Desired-state reconciliation

Programme Control/BRAIN operates increasingly as:

`CANONICAL DESIRED STATE ↔ OBSERVED REALITY → DRIFT → RECONCILE / FAIL CLOSED`.

Desired state comes from Founder Decisions, current authority, WBS/contracts, TEST KING/HEIR control and accepted proof requirements. Observed state comes from fresh GitHub/runtime/source/agent/external evidence. Branch names, recency and prose status are not authority. Material drift becomes explicit work or an explicit accepted exception; never silent divergence.

## 9. Evaluator-driven compounding

Use GOLD EVOLVE / evaluator-optimizer loops only where the fitness function is trustworthy enough to prevent optimisation toward the wrong target. Suitable early domains include bounded code optimisation, tests, retrieval/query strategies, deterministic transformations, accessibility/performance and other measurable contracts.

Never autonomously evolve or promote Canon, ecological truth, partner status, public claims or irreversible strategy.

Open research/strategy may use bounded hypothesis tournaments:

`GENERATE DIVERSE HYPOTHESES → SOURCE-CHECK → ADVERSARIAL CRITIQUE/DEBATE → RANK → EVOLVE → META-REVIEW → REAL-WORLD/HUMAN TEST`.

A hypothesis remains a hypothesis until evidence and authority promote it.

## 10. Standards-first

Before custom commodity infrastructure, assess established standards/primitives. Where a concrete need is proven, prefer compatible provenance, spatiotemporal interoperability, observability and media-provenance primitives over proprietary reinvention. Current candidate families include W3C PROV, STAC, OpenTelemetry and C2PA/Content Credentials.

Operating sequence: `ADOPT → PROFILE → EXTEND → PROVE → STANDARDISE NEW 4PLANET SEMANTICS ONLY WHERE NECESSARY`.

Do not force a migration merely because a standard exists.
