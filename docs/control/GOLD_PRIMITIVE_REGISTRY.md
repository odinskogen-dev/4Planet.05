# 4PLANET GOLD PRIMITIVE REGISTRY

Status: controlled working registry under the canonical GOLD / WORLD CLASS authority.  
Purpose: prevent local design/code patterns from becoming accidental system standards.

## Maturity states

- **PROVEN SHARED** — explicitly system-level or proven in at least two materially different user contexts without hierarchy, truth, mobile, accessibility, performance or maintenance regression.
- **PROVISIONAL SHARED** — strong reusable candidate; transfer evidence still incomplete.
- **PRODUCT-LOCAL** — intentionally specific to one product/journey/domain.
- **REJECTED** — tested and not suitable for reuse; preserve reason.
- **DEPRECATED** — formerly used; no new adoption.

A component name, repeated code or attractive screenshot is not proof of reuse quality.

## Promotion rule

To change a primitive to **PROVEN SHARED**, record:
1. source/first implementation;
2. at least one materially different transfer target unless explicit Founder/system authority already establishes it;
3. desktop + 390/430 rendered evidence;
4. hierarchy and next-action result;
5. accessibility/reduced-motion result where applicable;
6. truth/source implications;
7. performance/maintenance impact;
8. exact SHAs;
9. Founder decision only when the choice is genuinely taste/brand-ambiguous.

## Rejection / retirement rule

A rejected or deprecated pattern is never deleted from history merely to make the registry look clean. Record why it failed so future agents do not regenerate it under a new name.
