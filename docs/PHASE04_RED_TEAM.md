## Executive result

Phase 04 is materially stronger than the Phase 03 standalone lab because the distinctive behaviours now live inside the One Interface codebase.

The strongest prospective brand asset is not colour, underscore, 4-code or typography alone. It is the repeated product behaviour:

`LIFE / PLACE → RELATIONSHIP → SOURCE-AWARE CHANGE → CREDIBLE ROLE → PROOF → RETURN`

The remaining weaknesses are now concentrated rather than architectural:

1. real human comprehension/recall evidence does not exist yet;
2. the selected Oslofjorden front door still lacks a rights-clean high-end Oslofjorden documentary hero;
3. the source-backed Oslofjorden semantic identity exists, but display/query/scientific geometry must remain use-case-specific rather than invented as one universal polygon;
4. local source-aware Oslofjorden LIFE records, actor/solution intelligence, production Follow, action and outcome/proof remain incomplete.
