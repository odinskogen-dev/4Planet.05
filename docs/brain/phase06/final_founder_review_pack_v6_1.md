## 2. WHAT THE HUMAN REVIEW FOUND

Four high-information answers were actually reviewed by the founder and were broadly accepted as good enough to continue. No structured per-card PASS labels were supplied or inferred. The material finding was that uncertainty was sometimes presented too tersely as keyword-style caveats.

## 3. WHAT WAS REPAIRED

`UNCERTAINTY_PRESENTATION_V1` now requires material uncertainty to explain:

1. what is uncertain;
2. why / what evidence is missing;
3. why it matters for the decision;
4. what remains supportable.

This was implemented in the deterministic answer/review presentation layer. Context Pack v4.0 and the truth schema were deliberately retained.

## Strategic advantage assessment

**Proven in bounded cases:** connected provenance, implementation linkage, contradiction preservation, explicit unknowns and reusable Context Packs create more structured decision context than a list of search results.

**Plausible but not globally proven:** the durable 4PLANET advantage is a connected, traceable decision-intelligence layer spanning planetary systems → problems → solutions → implementations → actors → places → evidence → outcomes → action.

**Still aspirational:** expert-trusted global coverage, continuously federated intelligence, broad public decision use and superiority across many domains at scale.

The thesis therefore survives Phase 06, but only as a bounded proof plus a defensible strategic direction — not yet as a globally demonstrated moat.
