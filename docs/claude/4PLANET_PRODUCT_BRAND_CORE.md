## 1. WHAT 4PLANET IS

North Star:

**Help bring the living planet back toward balance so humans and the rest of life can thrive together.**

Category direction:

**LIVING PLANET INTELLIGENCE**

Public brand line:

**4PLANET_ For a Living Planet.**

4PLANET is not one environmental campaign and not a collection of unrelated apps. It is a connected product and operating system that links:

`UNDERSTANDING → DECISION → INCENTIVE → ACTION → PROOF → LEARNING`

Standing architecture law:

**Separate worlds. Shared infrastructure. Controlled depth.**

The public products should feel distinct enough to be genuinely useful, while becoming stronger because they share identity, sources, place/taxon/context and truth infrastructure.

## 4. VISUAL GRAMMAR

Stable master surfaces:

- pure white;
- pure black.

Brand blue:

`#2E2EFF`

Domain colours:

- OCE4N_ / PLANET: `#2E2EFF`
- E4RTH_: `#3AE86F`
- S4PIENS_: `#FF4D22`
- 4CULTURE_: `#FF5ACD`

### Single-accent law

Default 4PLANET/product context may use brand blue against pure white or black.

Inside a Domain/Mission, the active Domain colour becomes the one intentional interface accent. Do not allow brand blue plus several Domain colours to compete as decorative UI noise.

Images can contain natural colour. This law governs intentional interface colour.

### Typography

**Instrument Sans** — brand/display, major product identity, hero statements, large headlines.

**DM Sans** — body, editorial reading, navigation, functional UI.

**Fragment Mono** — evidence, metadata, dates, coordinates, source/status labels, technical identifiers.

Avoid random font drift. No arbitrary serif system, generic Inter substitution or novelty typeface when the Brand OS already supplies a role.

### Composition

Prefer:

- exact grids;
- strong hierarchy;
- generous spatial rhythm;
- restrained line work;
- meaningful negative space;
- intentional image crops;
- progressive disclosure;
- fewer stronger objects rather than many weak cards.

Premium does not mean sparse regardless of utility. Dense information is allowed when the human job requires it, but hierarchy must make the density legible.

## 5. IMAGERY + VISUAL STORYTELLING

Priority hierarchy:

1. real documentary imagery directly relevant to the story/object;
2. official/scientific/source imagery;
3. maps/data/diagrams when they explain something real;
4. clearly editorial illustration;
5. generic context imagery only when necessary.

Do not use a beautiful nature image when the product really needs documentary or evidentiary specificity.

Every material image should have a known provenance/rights state. Never invent a licence, photographer, source or usage right.

For editorial/feature surfaces, images should carry information, atmosphere or narrative progression — not simply occupy space.

## 6. MOTION + INTERACTION

Motion is a 4PLANET signature only when it creates orientation, continuity, reveal, relationship or felt quality.

Use motion:

- calmly;
- with physical coherence;
- as progressive disclosure;
- to maintain spatial context;
- to make complex systems understandable;
- with reduced-motion support.

Avoid:

- gratuitous parallax;
- decorative particle systems;
- fake network arcs;
- attention-seeking animation;
- UI that delays useful action;
- animation that compromises performance.

Performance is Human Gold, not engineering housekeeping.

## 7. PRODUCT / UX LAWS

### Useful first. Truthful always.

The user should quickly accomplish a real job. Truth and source depth must survive simplification.

### Human understanding before internal architecture

Never expose programme structure merely because the backend contains it.

### One primary question / job per view

A strong screen can contain depth, but it should have a legible dominant human purpose.

### Progressive disclosure

Lead with the answer, object, organism, place or useful action. Let evidence and system depth open beneath it.

### REDUCE BEFORE GENERATE

`DELETE → PRIORITISE → CONSOLIDATE → HUMANISE → TEST → REFINE`

Do not solve clutter by adding another explanatory layer.

### Evaluation clocks

Relevant product views should work at:

- 5 seconds — what is this / why should I care?
- 30 seconds — what can I do here?
- 3 minutes — can I discover meaningful depth?
- expert depth — can I inspect sources, limitations and evidence?

### Mobile first-class

Mobile is not a compressed desktop afterthought. Touch, safe areas, scroll, panels, map movement, typography and state restoration must remain high quality.

### Accessibility is product quality

Keyboard/focus, semantics, contrast, motion preferences and legibility are part of premium quality.

## 8. PUBLIC VOICE / COPY

Default public language is English, with British English conventions unless the product/task specifies another locale.

Voice should be:

- clear;
- exact;
- confident without hype;
- intelligent but understandable;
- human rather than institutional;
- editorial rather than marketing-heavy;
- curious about the world rather than self-obsessed;
- concrete rather than slogan-stacked;
- emotionally literate without melodrama.

Prefer the world, organism, place, human problem or useful question as subject. Do not make every paragraph about 4PLANET.

Avoid:

- startup hype;
- unsupported superlatives;
- generic sustainability language;
- guilt-first environmental messaging;
- inflated claims of impact, partnership or operational maturity;
- technical jargon as public identity;
- repeated internal system names when normal language works better.

Public copy must distinguish what is known, interpreted, planned, unavailable and not yet proven.

Do not label periodic/historical data as real-time without evidence.

UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.

## 9. ATLAS PRODUCT DOCTRINE

ATLAS is useful to people who have never heard of 4PLANET.

The map is the product.

The globe should feel like:

- planetary observation;
- public intelligence;
- scientific instrument;
- high design.

Not a control panel with a map behind it.

Core human questions:

- WHAT LIVES WHERE?
- WHAT EXACTLY AM I CLICKING ON?
- WHAT IS HAPPENING HERE?
- HOW IS THIS PLACE CHANGING?
- WHERE IS HUMAN PRESSURE COMING FROM?
- CAN I RETURN TO WHAT I CARE ABOUT?

Truth classes must remain distinct:

1. SOURCE DATA;
2. ATLAS-DERIVED CONTEXT;
3. 4PLANET CONTEXT.

Do not turn occurrence records into live positions, complete range, abundance or population estimates.

At global scale show patterns; regional scale clusters/areas; local scale individual records/features.

Source failure ≠ zero.

The right representation changes with scale.

## 10. SPECIES PRODUCT DOCTRINE

SPECIES must make life visible before metadata.

Strong profile order:

1. life-first visual identity;
2. common + scientific identity;
3. a short magnetic human explanation;
4. where it lives;
5. evidence/observations where available;
6. relationships to habitat, food, climate and other life;
7. pressures;
8. why it matters, without unsupported ecological-criticality claims;
9. connected ATLAS / Living Systems / Mission pathways;
10. sources, rights and limitations.

A species page should not feel like a prettier database record. It should help a non-expert meet a living organism, understand its world and then access evidence depth.

Current flagship design-target hypothesis: globally oriented, digitally fluent, visually driven non-expert adults roughly 25–45. Do not oversimplify for them; remove specialist friction while preserving intellectual depth.

## 16. CURRENT PREMIUM BRAND CONTROL

The durable direction above is supplemented by current Founder-approved premium controls that future product work must remember unless a fresher Founder decision supersedes them:

- the public product family should feel like one recognisable 4PLANET universe, not four unrelated apps;
- the Product Switcher should be quiet, precise and recognisably 4PLANET rather than a loud four-square app launcher;
- the principal header CTA is `JOIN 4PLANET` where that shared-header control is present;
- the global header is visually transparent when closed, hides cleanly on downward scroll and returns on upward scroll where the current shell supports that behaviour;
- default product context uses one intentional brand-blue accent against pure white or pure black;
- a Domain or Mission uses its one active Domain colour as the interface accent; brand blue and multiple Domain colours must not compete decoratively;
- life, place and clear human meaning should dominate over system labels, cards, technical implementation language and dashboard geometry;
- documentary photography and evidence-relevant imagery should outrank procedural/AI illustration when a truthful rights-safe real image exists and the Founder has directed documentary treatment;
- mobile quality is equal in importance to desktop quality;
- premium international quality includes interaction, accessibility, performance, copy, imagery, hierarchy and truth presentation — not visual polish alone.

A prior dense/boxed `Premium Narrative` implementation was explicitly rejected as a material visual regression. Narrative research may remain useful donor material, but dense boxed UI is not an accepted premium baseline merely because the copy or concept was good.

These controls are product/brand constraints, not a command to preserve every existing local UI choice. Claude should still challenge weak implementation and find better ways to express them.
