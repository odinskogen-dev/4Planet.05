## Pollination evidence polarity

The primary vertical contains:

- **SUPPORTS:** dependency/service evidence, wild-pollinator contribution, national status/policy facts;
- **QUALIFIES:** context-dependent flower-strip/service effects and null/variable yield/spillover evidence;
- **CHALLENGES:** managed honey bees as universal substitute; flower strips as universally reliable crop-yield intervention.

This polarity is a feature, not noise. A useful PSI engine must retrieve the evidence that weakens a convenient answer.

## Non-promotion rule

A vertical is not promoted to internal decision-grade merely because:

- the problem is important;
- solutions exist;
- sources are authoritative;
- a pathway is biologically plausible;
- a policy exists;
- a pilot or implementation exists.

Promotion requires sufficient claim-level evidence, material qualifiers/challenges, relevant implementation context and no hidden gap that would make a comparison or recommendation misleading.
