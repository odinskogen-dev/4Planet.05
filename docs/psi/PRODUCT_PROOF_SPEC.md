# 4PLANET PSI — Public Product Proof Specification v1.0

**Status:** INTERNAL PRODUCT SPEC / NO PUBLIC RELEASE

## Proof objective

Demonstrate, within roughly two to three minutes, that 4PLANET can move from a living-system question to a sourced, bounded action-intelligence view without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.

Primary proof vertical: **Pollination → Food**.

## Entry

User searches or opens **Pollination**.

The interface resolves a shared Living Systems / BRAIN context rather than creating a new page-owned identity.

## 1 — WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

Show the dependency chain:

`Pollinators → Pollination → Crop reproduction / production → Food systems / dietary diversity / livelihoods`

Display evidence state and source date beside material claims.

Boundary copy must make clear that crop-type dependency, production volume and economic value are different quantities.

## 2 — WHAT DEPENDS ON IT?

Show related:

- crops / food-production systems;
- ecological functions;
- human dependencies;
- linked living systems.

Western honey bee may appear as one taxon example, never as a proxy for all pollinators.

## 3 — WHAT THREATENS IT?

Show supported pressure families such as:

- habitat loss / simplification;
- pesticide exposure;
- climate and phenological pressures where evidence supports the context;
- other place-specific pressures only when sourced.

Pressure ≠ universal causal magnitude.

## 4 — WHAT MAY HELP?

Reuse existing PSI solution identities.

Candidate cards include:

- flower-rich field margins / habitat mosaics;
- pesticide-risk reduction integrated with habitat planning;
- integrated pest management;
- broader ecological restoration / stewardship pathways.

Each card must expose:

- semantic relevance;
- evidence availability;
- implementation evidence availability;
- important qualifiers / challenged propositions;
- no “best” badge unless comparative evidence supports it.

## 5 — WHAT DOES THE EVIDENCE SAY?

Three-column evidence polarity is the core trust proof:

### SUPPORTS
Examples of pollination dependency, wild-pollinator contribution and biodiversity-mediated service evidence.

### QUALIFIES
Context-dependent effects, transferability limits and null/variable yield findings.

### CHALLENGES
Explicitly rejected overclaims such as:

- managed honey bees universally substitute for wild pollinators;
- flower strips reliably increase crop yield everywhere.

The UI must never average these into one confidence score.

## 6 — WHERE?

Minimum ATLAS proof:

**Norway — administrative Place context**

Show the Norwegian cross-sectoral action plan for wild pollinating insects as a public-decision record connected to the pollinator problem.

Allowed:

- country geometry / administrative Place;
- source and policy period;
- related pressure / intervention families.

Not allowed:

- invented field coordinates;
- claim that the plan produced ecological improvement;
- query-area membership presented as Place truth.

## 7 — WHO?

Where source-backed, show responsible/public actors attached to the decision or implementation record.

Actor association must not imply partnership with 4PLANET.

## 8 — WHAT IS STILL MISSING?

Show gaps as a first-class section:

- implementation-level outcome evidence;
- normalized economics / cost-effect observations;
- geographic transferability;
- long-term monitoring;
- evidence conflicts / uncertainty.

Unknown ≠ no.

## 9 — WHAT COULD 4PLANET DO?

Only show legitimate internal opportunity hypotheses, for example:

- improve evidence linking;
- surface credible implementations;
- connect Place, actors and measurement;
- create a mission candidate after partner/proof gates.

Do not imply a mission, partner, funding need or impact unit already exists merely because the intelligence path exists.

## Required trust labels

- WORKING PROTOTYPE
- SOURCE-REPORTED / 4PLANET INTERPRETATION / INFERENCE
- REVIEW STATUS
- EVIDENCE STRENGTH
- LIMITATIONS
- LAST CHECKED
- NO OUTCOME EVIDENCE where appropriate

## Reuse across products

- **BRAIN:** canonical identity, claims, sources, assessments and graph edges.
- **Living Systems:** WHY / dependency / what may help.
- **ATLAS:** WHERE / spatial context.
- **DECISIONS:** policy and institutional context.
- **Mission Engine:** potential intervention opportunity only after gates.
- **IMPACT / PROOF:** later action/outcome path; not fabricated in this proof.

## Product acceptance

The proof succeeds when a normal user can answer:

1. Why pollination matters.
2. What depends on it.
3. What threatens it.
4. What may help.
5. Which claims are supported, qualified or challenged.
6. Where one real decision context exists.
7. What remains unknown.

without being given a false impression that 4PLANET has verified a universal solution or measured ecological outcome.
