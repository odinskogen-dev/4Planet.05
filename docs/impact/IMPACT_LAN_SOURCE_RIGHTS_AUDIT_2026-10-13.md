# 4PLANET IMPACT — Source & Rights Audit v0.1
Date: 2026-10-13
Scope: Living Action Network Gold Slice 01

## Rule
NATUREBRAIN may store source identity, provenance, factual claims, checked metrics and links without assuming a right to republish third-party editorial copy, photography, logos or video. Public product copy must be original synthesis. Third-party media is blocked unless a specific licence or permission is recorded.

## Media
Gold 01 uses existing 4PLANET rights-cleared imagery only, and every project tile labels it as an ISSUE-FAMILY VISUAL, NOT PROJECT EVIDENCE. Project-specific third-party imagery is not ingested.

## External actions
External donation/support links are discovery routes only. They do not create a 4PLANET contribution, receipt, delivery record, outcome, partner status or attribution claim.

## Review semantics
- DISCOVERED: credible public record exists.
- SOURCE-CHECKED: identity/activity/geography/core fact checked against named sources.
- EXTERNALLY VETTED: named external institution applied a defined review process.
- 4PLANET REVIEWED: reserved for a published 4PLANET method; not used in Gold 01.
- PARTNER: requires a real current agreement; public listing is not partnership.
- OUTCOME EVIDENCED: requires evidence for the exact ecological outcome, not merely activity or delivery.

## Release gate
Any future source adapter must declare: canonical URL, source authority, rights/attribution posture, retrieval date, limitations, allowed fields, refresh cadence and whether data is source-reported or independently verified.
