# 4PLANET Magazine — International Editorial Gold Benchmark 01

Purpose: judge the reader experience against international editorial leaders without copying their voice or design language.

## Benchmarks studied — 24 Aug 2026

- The Guardian Long Read / Environment: scene-led openings, explicit argument, reported detail, tension between evidence and interpretation, longform pacing that keeps moving.
- The New Yorker: concrete human scene first, then widening context; precise explanatory passages embedded inside narrative rather than separated as system copy.
- WIRED Science/Climate: direct headlines, short standfirst promise, fast transition from news hook to mechanism, strong explanatory utility.
- Vogue Business sustainability: named people and institutions early, industry stakes, concrete operational consequences, expert quotes and trade-offs rather than generic sustainability framing.
- National Geographic pattern: visual encounter earns attention, then species/place/science context expands the meaning; imagery must carry documentary or explanatory value.
- BBC/TIME/Bloomberg/VICE/Monocle patterns are used as additional quality references for clarity, hierarchy, pace, human relevance and strong editing, not as templates to imitate.

## Reader-standard matrix

| Dimension | International gold behaviour | 4PLANET release rule |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Specific, memorable, earns curiosity | Concrete promise; no abstract system language or clickbait |
| Standfirst | What happened + why it matters + reason to continue | 1–3 sentences; no generic topic summary |
| First 30 sec | Reader enters scene, tension, person, place or surprising fact | No product architecture before story; narrative starts immediately |
| Reporting | Multiple evidence objects where the story makes material claims | Full source-reported features normally carry primary + method/independent context |
| Mechanism | Explains how the thing works, not only that it exists | Every feature must make at least one mechanism legible |
| Uncertainty | Limits are explicit without killing the story | Observation, claim, interpretation and unknown remain distinct |
| Human trace | Irregular, specific, reported, edited | Remove generic AI cadence, repeated antithesis and corporate abstraction |
| Visuals | Specific imagery, strong captions, documentary context | Correct image beats pretty image; generic context is last resort and labelled |
| Typography | Confident hierarchy, calm reading measure | Instrument Sans display; DM Sans reading/UI; Fragment Mono metadata |
| Mobile | Designed as a reader experience, not collapsed desktop | Headline, hero, standfirst and body rhythm inspected at 390/430 px |
| Trust | Byline, date, source and correction path are easy to find | Trust is present but never blocks the opening of a story |
| Recirculation | Related reading is editorially relevant, not generic | One useful next story/object; no CTA clutter |
| Reader value | Reader leaves knowing, seeing or understanding something new | Every paragraph must advance story, mechanism, evidence, context or consequence |

## 4PLANET editorial identity

Human first. Curious. Intelligent. Visually literate. Scientifically honest. Slightly provocative only when earned. Never preachy.

The signature is not louder design. It is the combination of living-world curiosity, systems literacy, evidence discipline, exceptional visual taste and a clear path from one vivid object to the wider system around it.

## Hard rejection tests

Reject or rework if a feature:

1. could be replaced by a competent press-release summary without losing much value;
2. opens with abstract framing before a concrete reason to care;
3. repeats the same rhetorical construction across paragraphs;
4. rests on one interested organisation for all material claims without a visible boundary;
5. uses generic nature imagery where documentary, source, map or data material is reasonably obtainable;
6. spends more time explaining 4PLANET than explaining the world;
7. looks good on desktop but becomes dense, broken or visually empty on mobile;
8. passes code QA while still failing editorial judgement.
