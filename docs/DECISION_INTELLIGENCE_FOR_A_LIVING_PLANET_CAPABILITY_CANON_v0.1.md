# 4PLANET_ DECISION INTELLIGENCE FOR A LIVING PLANET — CAPABILITY CANON v0.1

**Status:** Founder-directed internal capability canon. Not a fifth product. Not a public brand change. No production or automated-decision authority.

## North star

4PLANET should not only describe the living planet. It should help humans make better-informed decisions for it while preserving evidence, uncertainty, trade-offs and provenance.

Canonical capability flow:

`BRAIN → UNDERSTANDING → DECISION INTELLIGENCE → ACTION → MEASUREMENT → LEARNING`

This extends — and does not replace — the existing product architecture.

## Relationship to the existing system

- **BRAIN** owns canonical identities, relationships, claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, assessments and implementation/outcome truth.
- **LIVING SYSTEMS** is the primary understanding interface: what exists, what it does, what depends on it, what threatens it and what may help.
- **Solution Intelligence** supplies pathways/interventions/offerings and implementation evidence; `ADDRESSES` never means effective.
- **ATLAS** supplies explicit Place/jurisdiction/query-area/observation/implementation/range/modelled-area context; it is not a second graph.
- **Decision Intelligence** is a derived/runtime capability that assembles bounded Context Packs and Decision Packs for an explicit question, actor, objective, constraints and Place.
- **IMPACT** may later execute/report separately released actions and outcomes; a Decision Pack does not create delivery or impact claims.
- **AI** may resolve questions, select bounded context and explain structured evidence. AI output is not canonical truth and may not silently promote inference into BRAIN.

## Decision Pack v1

A Decision Pack may contain, where relevant:

- decision question, actor/user type, objective and constraints;
- Place/jurisdiction and evidence scope;
- problem frame and living/human-system dependencies;
- causal pressures/drivers;
- options: pathways, interventions, offerings and implementations;
- evidence for, qualifiers and challenges;
- expected outcomes versus observed outcomes;
- economics where credible;
- time horizon, maturity and transferability;
- co-benefits, risks and trade-offs;
- uncertainty and knowledge/implementation/actor/capability gaps;
- next information needed;
- possible next actions.

Every field must be classifiable as one of:

`FACT · SOURCE_REPORTED_CLAIM · 4PLANET_ASSESSMENT · INFERENCE · UNKNOWN`

## Decision-question taxonomy v1

`UNDERSTAND · DEPENDENCY · CAUSE · INTERVENTION · COMPARE · EVIDENCE · CONFLICT · PLACE · IMPLEMENTATION · OUTCOME · ECONOMICS · TRANSFERABILITY · ACTOR · GAP · PRIORITISATION · ACTION`

## Option comparison

Decision Intelligence may compare options across explicit dimensions, but must not hide normative choices in a global score.

`LENS_SENSITIVITY_V1` uses explicit priority dimensions and pairwise relationships only. It may surface `DOMINATES`, `DOMINATED`, `TRADE_OFF`, `TIE_OR_INDETERMINATE` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` **within that declared lens and represented dimensions**. These labels are not recommendations.

No aggregate score exists.

## Hard truth boundaries

- evidence ≠ judgement;
- relevance ≠ effectiveness;
- solution ≠ proven solution;
- implementation ≠ outcome;
- expected outcome ≠ observed outcome;
- modelled ≠ measured;
- policy ≠ result;
- actor ≠ partner;
- Source Registry ≠ immutable Source Record;
- database absence ≠ real-world absence;
- global/transferred evidence ≠ local evidence;
- decision support ≠ automated decision-making;
- pairwise lens dominance ≠ universal best option;
- AI output ≠ canonical truth.

## Place evidence states

A Decision Pack must expose one of:

`GLOBAL · LOCAL · TRANSFERRED · NO_LOCAL_EVIDENCE`

and preserve the distinction between:

`PLACE · JURISDICTION · QUERY_AREA · OBSERVATION_LOCATION · IMPLEMENTATION_LOCATION · TAXON_RANGE · ECOSYSTEM_EXTENT · MODELLED_AREA · ADMINISTRATIVE_CONTEXT`.

## Capability release rule

Decision Intelligence may be described internally as a strategic capability now. It should not become a fifth product or replace locked public category/brand language without a separate founder decision after:

1. full-corpus physical runtime proof;
2. at least one working end-to-end product proof;
3. human/expert evaluation;
4. evidence that the wording improves understanding rather than creating category confusion.
