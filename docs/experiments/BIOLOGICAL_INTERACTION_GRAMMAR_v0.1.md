**Design thesis:** **APPLE × LIVING ORGANISMS = 4PLANET.**

## Purpose

Use biological mechanisms as interaction, motion, information, spatial-organisation and software-behaviour references. Do not use biomimicry as decorative nature styling. Abstract the mechanism first, then test whether the translation improves comprehension, usability, distinctiveness or resilience.

Existing premium typography, black/white master surfaces, one-active-accent rule, cinematic imagery, source/truth architecture, accessibility and founder judgement remain authoritative.

## Experiment 01 — S4PIENS FOOD tropism

### Baseline

Source branch: `sandbox/s4piens-food-human-systems-atlas`.

Experiment branch: `experiment/biomimetic-s4piens-food-tropism-20260821`.

The original S4PIENS sandbox and the existing FOOD Gold Journey are preserved. The experiment adds a new Human Systems ingress before the existing deep journey.

### Mechanisms under test

1. **BI-07 Tropism:** selecting `FOOD_` creates a relevance field around Homo sapiens.
2. **BI-04 Homeostasis:** state transition `REST → SENSE → RESPOND → SETTLE`.
3. **BI-02 Vascular grammar:** FOOD relevance paths branch into related system nodes.
4. **BI-03 Context propagation:** S4PIENS orange becomes more active only where FOOD context becomes active.

### Nodes pulled toward FOOD context

- Agriculture
- Pollination
- Soil
- Freshwater
- Fertiliser
- Transport
- Climate
- Species
- Companies
- Solutions

Lower-relevance contexts such as `F4SHION_` and generic `SHIPPING` remain visible but recede outward.

### Truth boundary

Spatial proximity and line weight in this experiment represent **interface relevance for navigation**. They do not claim ecological causal strength, scientific dependency magnitude, population status or verified outcome.

### Acceptance questions

- Does the user understand that FOOD is embedded in a wider human system before entering the journey?
- Does the spatial reorganisation make relevance easier to perceive than a static menu or card grid?
- Does motion feel calm and precise rather than decorative?
- Can a keyboard user operate the focus controls?
- Does `prefers-reduced-motion` remove non-essential transitions?
- Does the experience remain legible at 390×844, 430×932 and 1440×900?
- Does the existing FOOD Gold Journey remain intact after the new ingress?

## Safety and preservation rules

1. Additive experiments only; no automatic main merge or production replacement.
2. Preserve the exact pre-experiment source branch/commit and rollback path.
3. No biological metaphor may invent relationships, confidence, causality or ecological outcomes.
4. Shared Product Context and canonical identities remain authoritative.
5. Source Record, Observation, Signal, Interpretation, Pressure, Solution, Contribution, Delivery, Proof and Outcome remain distinct.
6. Motion must improve hierarchy, state or comprehension; otherwise remove it.
7. Reduced motion, keyboard/focus, mobile touch and performance are mandatory acceptance axes.
8. Record expected mechanism, observed result, performance/accessibility cost and learning before cross-product promotion.
