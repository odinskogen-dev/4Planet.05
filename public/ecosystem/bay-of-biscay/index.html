SURVEY EFFORT

Measure the work.

Primary proof is effort: hours, distance and route geometry. Sightings add biological context; they are not the success metric by themselves.

PRESSURE

Read the place.

Shipping, noise, fishing activity, climate and habitat conditions belong beside survey observations as context — never as unsupported causal claims.

MONITORING LOGIC

Route → effort → observation → evidence.

The ecosystem card keeps four objects separate: the ferry corridor, survey effort, individual observations and broader ecological interpretation. That separation is the basis for credible reporting later.

SOURCE SEAMS

Evidence, not decoration.

ORCA survey records, species identity sources and marine context layers can be attached independently so unknowns remain explicit.

ACTION HANDOFF

Fund monitoring, not a promise.

A future funding unit can support verified survey effort on this corridor. Delivery evidence must remain separate from ecological outcome claims.
