Ancient warfare is more than a contest of numbers. Position, discipline, morale and resources shape the outcome. ROMA gives you command of the field — and responsibility for what you build behind it.

From a growing settlement to the moment two formations collide, the game explores what it takes to hold a Republic together.

01 — Command

The field is yours to shape.

Lead regiments through tactical formations, terrain, morale and live engagements. The battle responds to decisions, not a scripted sequence.

02 — Build

A Republic needs more than soldiers.

Gather resources, establish defences and build a settlement that can sustain a campaign. The choices beyond the battlefield matter.
