01 / The experience

A life beneath
the surface.

WILD: ORCA explores the ocean as an experience, not a collection of facts. Swim with a pod, follow underwater prey, move between open water and the ice edge, and discover how much an animal's world can change your perspective.

03 / The world behind the game

Belong to
something wild.

Movement, family and encounters form the heart of the game. Real marine ecology inspires the setting, while play remains the primary purpose. Species information is reviewed separately; this is not a scientifically validated simulation.
