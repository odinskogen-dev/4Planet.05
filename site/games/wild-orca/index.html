In WILD: ORCA, movement is freedom and family is survival. Glide through Arctic waters, communicate with your pod and experience life as an ocean predator.

Explore marine life in a world inspired by real species and habitats. It is designed to be a compelling game first.

01 — Move

Feel the ocean move with you.

Discover the weight and grace of an orca: swim, dive, accelerate and surface. Movement is the heart of the experience.

02 — Together

The pod changes everything.

Coordinate with family in hunts and encounters. Learn when to follow, when to lead and how connections shape life at sea.

03 — Project facts

The ocean is alive.

WILD: ORCA is a playable wildlife adventure with swimming, hunting and pod cooperation. The experience takes inspiration from real marine life, but gameplay is not a verified educational outcome.

The linked public 4PLANET build may be older than internal development. Species facts require review before use as validated learning material.
