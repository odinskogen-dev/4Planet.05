01 / About the game

A world beneath the surface.

Swim through cold water, follow your pod, dive beneath the ice and encounter marine life from the perspective of a killer whale. WILD: ORCA begins with an animal's movement and the relationships that make life in the ocean possible.

03 / The experience

The ocean is more than a backdrop.

Movement, survival, hunting and pod coordination form the experience. The public demo is hosted on 4PLANET and may differ from the latest internal candidate. Marine facts are under review and should not be treated as validated educational outcomes.
