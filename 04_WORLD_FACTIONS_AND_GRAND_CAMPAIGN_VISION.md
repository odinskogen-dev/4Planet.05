# THE LONG-TERM GAME — HISTORICAL WORLD, GRAND STRATEGY, REAL BATTLES
VISION, NOT FEATURE COMPLETENESS. Claude may revise architecture, scope and timing.

## Locked direction
ROMA is a historical strategy franchise with real-time tactical battles, authentic historic geography, castles/forts/towns, settlement-building and civic economies, resource exploitation and commerce, supply and warfare, territory, treaties, loyalties, dynamic rulers and commanders, historical campaigns with chosen start dates, naval operations, and player-made alternative history. The existing Claude ROMA Challenger tactical battle technology is valuable and protected.
A continent-sized physical base map should be chosen EARLY for stable coordinates and future expansion, but only one region should become richly playable first; DO NOT ship a mostly empty 'huge game' as if it were finished.

## Geographic extent in current prototype
-18 to +70 degrees longitude, +10 to +61 degrees latitude: western Atlantic approach and Iberia; British Isles and continental Europe; entire Mediterranean basin; North Africa and Nile; Greece, Balkans, Anatolia; Levant and Mesopotamia; Iranian plateau and towards Bactria/Central Asia.
The current geographic extent is a convenience, not yet an accepted canonical world bound. Evaluate eastward extension beyond 70 E and north/south bounds when selecting genuine projection/terrain architecture. An actual strategic game should not use country boundaries from the 21st century.

## Factions for a grounded 216 BCE world (period appropriate; research details)
1. Roman Republic and unequal Italian allied peoples / city communities.
2. Carthaginian state and dependent/allied Punic settlements, Iberian and African forces.
3. Numidian dynastic spheres, including Massylii/Masaesyli, shifting alliances.
4. Macedonian kingdom under Philip V (and neighbouring Greek states/leagues); distinguish separate Greek city states.
5. Ptolemaic kingdom in Egypt and adjacent territories.
6. Seleucid kingdom in the eastern Mediterranean and Near East under Antiochus III, with changing control.
7. Anatolian kingdoms, including Pontus, Pergamon and others where date-supported; distinct Galatian communities.
8. Independent Gaulish/Celtic groups in northern Italy and beyond, not one united Gaul.
9. Diverse Iberian communities and tribes, not one unified Iberian kingdom.
10. Illyrian powers, Thracian kingdoms/communities and the far western/northern frontier.
11. Parthian/Arsacid and Greco-Bactrian spheres farther east, with carefully checked 216 BCE control and chronology.
12. Independent North African communities and tribes, not modern-state borders.

Important: the Achaemenid 'Persian Empire' no longer exists in 216 BCE. Egyptian 'Empire' here means the Ptolemaic kingdom, not the New Kingdom pharaohs. The Han dynasty's core is outside the current map. Do not blend centuries or present these factions with one-size-fits-all Roman mechanics. Represent dependent cities and alliances, not only independent states.

## Expansion eras (examples, each needs date-specific research)
Early Republic and Italian unification; First Punic War (264–241 BCE); Second Punic War (218–201 BCE); Macedonian/Syrian Wars and eastern Mediterranean (2nd c BCE); Late Republic and Caesar (1st c BCE); Augustan transition (late 1st c BCE); early Principate (1st–2nd c CE). New starting dates require updated borders, factions, assets, units, rulers, roads and technologies, not global stat adjustments. Separate Medieval Kingdoms/Crusades franchise experiments are NOT part of this ROMA handoff.

## Grand strategic player loop
Select a historical start date and faction > locate pressing frontier/supply/diplomatic problem > choose policy/building/economy priorities > raise/maintain forces > manoeuvre armies and navies through sourced terrain and contested corridors > conduct sieges, skirmishes and tactical battles in original Claude engine > resolve control, recruitment, trade, treaties, settlement prosperity, loyalty and dynamic history > continue into durable, replayable empire-scale campaign.
Every new mechanic should create a trade-off and be testable. Never copy copyrighted game assets, unique UI or protected compositions from competitors.

## Depth over sheer scale
Vertical slice sequence: Cannae/Canusium region > meaningful Campania/Apulia logistics > full southern Italy > peninsula/Sicily > western Mediterranean and Iberian/African theatres > Greek/eastern campaign > full planned world. Design stable geospatial IDs and data seams NOW, but don't populate placeholder empires or fake cities.
Potential companion technology: reuse 4PLANET ATLAS geospatial ideas only after proving export interfaces, licensing, performance and architecture fit. Do not couple ROMA deployment or code to 4PLANET production.

## Products not yet decided

Do not introduce paid currencies, accounts, live servers or microtransactions here without a distinct approved product decision.
