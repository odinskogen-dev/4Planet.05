S4PIENS — Human Systems Intelligence

S4PIENS is a public 4PLANET product for understanding the human systems that shape everyday life and the living planet. It looks at value chains, incentives, infrastructure, production, consumption and other relationships that are difficult to understand when each actor is viewed in isolation.

The product is distinct from 4SAPIEN. 4SAPIEN is built around an individual person and private user context; S4PIENS is the wider systems lens. The goal is to connect source-grounded understanding of human systems with places, living systems, companies and practical opportunities for improvement.

This is an evolving public prototype. Where evidence is incomplete, uncertainty should remain visible rather than being replaced with confident assumptions.
